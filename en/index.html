How we work

Five decisions that define us

01

Designed like a product

Nobody reads the manual to start the washing machine. If software needs one for the basics, it is badly designed.

We come from product design, and a computer system is a product too: it has to make sense the first time. So we design it with the people who will use it, at their workplace, and test it with them before we hand it over.

02

Traceable to the source

Every published figure can be traced back to the official file, with automatic checks before anything is published.

03

On top of what exists

We connect read-only to your data and systems; anything new is stored separately. Nothing breaks.

04

People in charge

Agents propose and a person approves: nothing with a real effect happens without approval, in line with the EU AI Act.

05

Small-business cost

Static websites and AI that costs cents per query: at MITÜMI, the AI layer was estimated at €6.70 a month.
